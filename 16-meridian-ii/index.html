How we work

How a project runs, from α to ω.

We work in short rounds and show you something working after each one. Everything we build stays yours.

  1. α

    Listen

    You walk us through the reports you rely on and the questions they can’t answer.

  2. 2

    Map

    We go through your systems and spreadsheets and tell you what the data can support today.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build in short rounds, each ending with something you can open and check.

  4. ω

    Automate

    Whatever still gets done by hand each week goes on a schedule, with an alert when it fails.

When the project is finished, we are still there to maintain it and keep it running.

Our name and symbol

A Greek word and the first analogue computer.

The name says what we look for. The symbol shows the machine that first looked for it, piece by piece.

νόημα

The name

In Greek, when something makes sense, you say it has νόημα. That is what a business wants from its data.

The gear

The main wheel of the Antikythera mechanism, the first analogue computer. For us: the machinery we build.

The dial

The ring around the gear, marked with four dots, is where the mechanism showed its answer. For us: where you read yours.

Questions

Common questions.

Our data is a mess. Is that a problem?

No. Tidying it is part of the job. The first step is working out what your data can support today.

Do you work with the tools we already have?

Yes. We start with what you have, spreadsheets included, and suggest a new tool only when the current one is holding the work back.

What can you automate?

Whatever your team does by hand on a schedule: sending invoices, building reports, running an analysis, sending emails.

How do we start?

Email us to arrange a meeting. Tell us in a line or two what you want to fix, and we’ll set up a call, in English or Greek.
