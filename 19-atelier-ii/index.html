How we work

How a project runs, from α to ω.

We work in short rounds and show you something working after each one. Everything we build stays yours.

  1. α

    Listen

    You walk us through the reports you rely on and the questions they can’t answer.

  2. 2

    Map

    We go through your systems and spreadsheets and tell you what the data can support today.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build in short rounds, each ending with something you can open and check.

  4. ω

    Automate

    Whatever still gets done by hand each week goes on a schedule, with an alert when it fails.

Our name and symbol

A Greek word and the first analogue computer.

νόημα

Nóima /ˈno.i.ma/ · Greek for “sense”, “meaning”

In Greek, when something makes sense, you say it has νόημα. That is what a business wants from its data, so we made it the name.

The gear

The main wheel of the Antikythera mechanism, the first analogue computer. For us, it stands for the machinery we build.

α to ω

The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet, top and bottom. From raw data to meaning, start to finish.

Moon and Sun

The mechanism followed both across the sky, the Moon left and the Sun right. For us, what you want to know.

The dial

The open ring around the wheel, marked with four dots, where the answer was read. For us, your reports and dashboards.

Together

The mechanism that read the sky and the Greek word for meaning. Together they say what we do with your data.

Found in a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera in 1901, a bronze mechanism of at least 30 gears predicted eclipses and the movements of the sky more than 2,000 years ago.

Questions

Common questions.

Our data is a mess. Is that a problem?

No. Tidying it is part of the job. The first step is working out what your data can support today.

Do you work with the tools we already have?

Yes. We start with what you have, spreadsheets included, and suggest a new tool only when the current one is holding the work back.

What can you automate?

Whatever your team does by hand on a schedule: sending invoices, building reports, running an analysis, sending emails.
