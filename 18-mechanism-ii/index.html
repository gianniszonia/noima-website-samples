Our name and symbol

A Greek word and the first analogue computer.

νόημα

The name

Nóima is the Greek word for sense or meaning. When something makes sense, Greeks say it has νόημα.

The gear

The main wheel of the Antikythera mechanism, the first analogue computer. For us it stands for the machinery we build.

The dial

The open ring around the gear, marked by four dots. On the mechanism, this is where the answer was read.

α to ω

Alpha sits at the top and omega at the bottom. Every project runs from raw data to meaning, start to finish.

Moon and Sun

The Moon on the left and the Sun on the right are what the mechanism tracked. For us: your questions.

Together

The mechanism that read the sky and the Greek word for meaning. Together they say what we do with data.

Found in a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera in 1901, a bronze mechanism of at least 30 gears predicted eclipses and the movements of the sky more than 2,000 years ago.
